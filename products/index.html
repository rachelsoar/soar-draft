Your Work

Workplace AI

Your meetings, transcribed, analyzed, and turned into action automatically. Workplace AI becomes part of your meeting routine and handles the busywork so your team can focus on the decisions that actually matter.

Every conversation is captured, every action item is assigned, and every commitment is tracked. The system remembers what was said, who said it, and what was promised — so nothing falls through the cracks and no one has to take notes again.

Your Community

Citizen Portal

Every public meeting, vote, and government decision at your fingertips — searchable, organized, unfiltered, and impossible to spin. Citizen Portal puts over a million hours of public record into a format that ordinary people can actually use.

Monitor your representatives the way they monitor you. Track how they vote, what they say, and whether they follow through. Government transparency should not require a law degree or a full-time research staff.

The Platform

Fleet of Agents

Every SOAR product comes with a fleet — a team of specialized AI agents that work together on your behalf, constantly learning and improving. Instead of one AI that tries to do everything, you get purpose-built agents coordinated by a single orchestration layer.

Each agent is trained on a specific domain and shares context with every other agent in the fleet. The result is work that gets better the more you use it, because the system remembers your patterns, your preferences, and your history across every interaction.

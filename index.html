Meet the founder

Paul Allen

Co-founder of Ancestry.com · Founder & CEO, SOAR

Paul co-founded Ancestry.com in 1997 — the platform that helped over 100 million people discover their family history. A sought-after keynote speaker and Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year, he's spent thirty years building technology that makes complex, deeply human things accessible to everyone.

With SOAR, he's building what he calls PURE AI — Personalized, Uplifting, Responsible, Ethical — across work, family, faith, community, and beyond.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does Soar actually do? add
Soar is an AI studio that builds products across work, family, community, and learning. Our flagship product, Workplace AI, turns meetings into structured knowledge and action items. But we also build tools for authors, lawyers, tradespeople, podcasters, families, educators, and public institutions — all powered by the same underlying AI platform.
Who founded Soar? add
Paul Allen, co-founder of Ancestry.com. He spent thirty years building technology that makes complex, deeply human things accessible to everyone. With Soar, he's applying that same philosophy to AI — building what he calls PURE AI: Personalized, Uplifting, Responsible, and Ethical.
What is "Bring Your Own Agents"? add
BYOA is our belief that every person should have access to their own AI agents — not just the company they work for. Your agents learn how you work, write, and think. They travel with you. They're yours. Learn more about the BYOA pledge →
What is a fleet of agents? add
Instead of one AI tool that tries to do everything, a fleet is a team of specialized agents that each handle a specific job — taking notes, drafting reports, tracking action items, coaching sales calls — and coordinate together. It's a new way to work with AI, and we're building it now. See what we're building →
How is Soar different from other AI companies? add
Most AI companies build tools for companies. We build tools for people. Our products are designed around your life — not just your job — and every one of them is built on the principle that AI should make you more capable, not more dependent. We don't sell your data, we don't serve advertisers, and we don't build general-purpose chatbots. We build specific products that do specific things well.
Can I partner with Soar or white-label a product? add
Yes. We work with organizations who want to bring Soar's AI capabilities to their own audience under their own brand — across any vertical. Schedule a conversation →

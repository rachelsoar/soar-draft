Founder & CEO

Paul Allen

Co-founder of Ancestry.com · Based in Utah & Missouri

Paul conceived Ancestry.com in 1996 and launched it as founding CEO in 1997. The platform has since helped over 100 million people discover their family history. A sought-after keynote speaker and Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year, he's spent three decades building technology that makes complex, deeply human things accessible to everyone.

"If I have one entrepreneurial superpower, it's asking questions at scale."

His Vision

With SOAR, Paul is building what he calls PURE AI — a framework for artificial intelligence that puts people first.
